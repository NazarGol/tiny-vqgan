# Research decisions (tiny VQGAN engine), one line each, newest last

- 2026-10-01 Work happens in the worktree `~/VQPAINT-research` on `research/tiny-vqgan`, branched from `web-spikes` (main is 36 commits behind it; PR #2 is pending). Product files are never edited here; model/data dirs are symlinked read-only from the main checkout.
- 2026-10-01 Minimum devices are in `web/DEVICES.md` (iPhone XR/11/SE2 on iOS 15+, 3 GB Android from 2020). Historic "13 mini" numbers in the product notes stay as records; new work follows DEVICES.md.
- 2026-10-01 Training data is generated on the fly from token grids only (no pixels on Kaggle): bank grids (6500 photos + 1500 paintings at 16×16), 32×32 grids from COCO/CelebA at 512 px encoded locally, uniform-random grids, and search-style mutations (palette samples, neighbour copies, block moves, swaps, mosaics). Targets are the original decoder's output computed in the kernel.
- 2026-10-01 Tiny decoder architecture: learned 16384×64 token table (PCA-initialised from the codebook) + stages at 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 resolution with widths 64,64,64,32,16 (variant A) or 64,64,48,24,12 (B), each stage = 3×3 conv (+nearest 2× upsample) and 2-conv residual blocks, ReLU, no norms. 1.6 M params ≈ 3.2 MB fp16. Ops are restricted to what one fragment-shader pass does (3×3 conv with zero padding, nearest upsample, residual add, ReLU).
- 2026-10-01 WebGL2 runtime: one RGBA16F texture per activation (channel groups of 4 tiled side by side), one draw per output channel group with that group's weights in a std140 uniform block (≤ 9.2 KB, fits the 16 KB minimum), weights fp16 on disk. First run matched PyTorch to the 8-bit output rounding (max err 0.002) in Chromium and WebKit; 256 px decode 12–15 ms on the M1 Pro.
- 2026-10-01 Token scorer: same kind of token table, the embedding grid resized bilinearly to 16×16 (the app resizes every crop to 256 px for CLIP), four conv stages (stride 2 after the first), 2×2 flatten → MLP 512. Trained with 1−cos plus a pair term on (grid, small mutation) pairs so small deltas are learned; evaluated by Pearson/Spearman per prompt and sign agreement of pair deltas.
- 2026-10-01 Real strokes for evaluation come from the app's own search run headlessly (web/research/gen_strokes.mjs, 40 prompts, realism 0.2–0.8, 6 s each) since the shots rooms are empty and real room ids are unknown.
